# NEXORA Roadmap

1. Foundation: API, web, worker, configuration, persistence, Docker, CI and tests.
2. Intelligence: research, task intelligence, specialist selection, briefs, prompt critique and model routing.
3. Agents: runtime, capability registry, tools and MCP.
4. Memory: projects, semantic retrieval, assets and account memory.
5. Media: raw editing, generated video, hybrid editing, audio, captions, thumbnails and QA.
6. Integrations: GitHub, Vercel, Google, YouTube, Instagram and LinkedIn.
7. Quality: evaluation, telemetry, audit and cost tracking.
8. Production: authentication, encrypted secrets, rate limits, sandboxing and durable workflows.
